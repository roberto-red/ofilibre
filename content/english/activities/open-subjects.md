Materials related to the call for recognition of open access course publication. This call, first launched for the 2022–2023 academic year, is carried out by Universidad Rey Juan Carlos to promote the creation of high-quality open educational resources by faculty, and to encourage their use in the courses they teach. The initiative is led by the Vice-Rectorates for Digital Transformation and Teaching Innovation, Campus Community, Culture and Sports, and Academic Planning and Faculty Development.
