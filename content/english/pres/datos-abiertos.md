More and more, open data is becoming a requirement in many research fields, a symptom of transparency, and a very useful resource in teaching and research. What advantages do they have? What problems and challenges do they pose? Where can I find open data? How can I make the data I produce open? During this session, we will try to address these and other questions, and we will initiate a debate on the relationship we want to have with open data as teachers and researchers.
