At OfiLibre, as advocates of open source, we use a computer running a **GNU/Linux** distribution. If you want to know how we replaced the previous operating system with the current one, we explain it in detail in our [latest guide](/en/guias/migracion-windows-a-linux). Whether you're looking to improve your device’s performance, strengthen your privacy, or simply make the leap to **free software**, this guide will give you the tools and knowledge needed to make the transition safely and easily.
