There are many free materials available on the Internet that you can reuse for your work, or in general for your own creations. But it's not always easy to find them, or even to recognize them. Welcome to the world of free works: let's see what they are, how we can recognize them, and how we can find them. We also talk about how to make your own works free, and what (positive and negative) implications this may have for you.

Among other topics, we cover the following:

* Copying and reusing is not (necessarily) bad
* What are free works?
* Can I use a free work in my projects?
* The main free licenses
* Finding free works: images
* Finding free works: audio
* Finding free works: videos
* Finding free works: other elements
* How to make my work free (and what I gain from it)
