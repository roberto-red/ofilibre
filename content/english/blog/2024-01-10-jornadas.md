In March 2024, we will have the third edition of the Free Culture Days of URJC. Last year, our leitmotiv was "closer than you think" and, boy, free culture is close! So close that it's always on top of us, in actions like the Sexenios criteria, and surely in the accreditations. That's why, for these third days, we believe that the phrase "you can't escape" is the most appropriate leitmotiv.

Save Wednesday, March 20, and Thursday, March 21, to share with OfiLibre team III Free Culture Days of URJC. You can't escape!

### What will we do during III days?

We will have activities such as workshops, lectures, and talks, with special guests, around free culture. We will see how to create free content, delve into free software, and open publications and data, without forgetting breaks to eat and have a coffee!

Over the next few weeks, we will complete the information and important dates (to receive contributions, to register, etc.) in this [entry in the University's event system](https://eventos.urjc.es/109643/detail/terceras-jornadas-de-cultura-libre-de-la-urjc.html)

We're waiting for you, this time you can't escape! :)
