A very common scenario in these times of distance learning is having recorded a class that took place via videoconference and wanting to turn it into a free video that we can upload to URJC TV or any video publishing platform (YouTube, Vimeo, PeerTube, etc.).

This video recipe focuses on this scenario. We start from a video that has been recorded using the option provided by Teams for this purpose and has become available on Stream (as usually happens with the configuration we have at URJC). Using Stream itself (to select the part of the video that interests us) and free tools (LibreOffice, OpenShot), we are going to produce a video similar to this one in the recipe, which allows students to better understand what they are going to see in it (thanks to a short header) and makes it clear that it is a free video that can be shared (thanks to a license and credits included at the end).
