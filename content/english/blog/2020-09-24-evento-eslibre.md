On September 18 and 19, the 2020 edition of the [esLibre](https://eslib.re/2020/) congress was held, entirely remotely due to the COVID-19 health crisis. This year the organization was in the hands of OfiLibre, with—among others—the support of other communities such as [Interferencias](https://interferencias.tech/), [LibreLabGRX](https://librelabgrx.cc/), [Wikimedia España](https://www.wikimedia.es/) and many other people.

The event was very well received, with more than 200 participants who were also very active on Twitter under the hashtag #eslibre2020.

Different channels were made available so that everyone could stay informed and had the possibility to create discussion spaces. All of this using free technologies such as [*BigBlueButton*](https://bbb.eslibre.urjc.es/b) and [*RocketChat*](https://rocket.eslibre.urjc.es/channel/ayuda).

The talks and activities were very varied and included highly interesting proposals from different communities, ranging from free software and technologies to free culture, as exemplified by the room created by Wikimedia España. In addition, the workshops provided a great way to put many skills into practice. [Here](https://eslib.re/2020/programa/) you can check the full eslibre 2020 program.

An immersive experience was possible thanks to the use of [Mozilla Hubs](https://hubs.mozilla.com/) technology, which allowed attendees to dive into a virtual world. It offered a closer way for congress participants to interact and was very well received.

Even so, if you were unable to attend and are interested, we would like to inform you that the talks will soon be published on URJC TV.
