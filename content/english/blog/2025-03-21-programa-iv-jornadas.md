The next 26 and 27 of March 2025 will take place the **Fourth Free Culture Days of the URJC**. They are conceived as a meeting point, training, and exchange of experiences on topics related to free culture at the University: open publication (of teaching materials, research, etc.), open science, open data, free software, etc.

It will feature **expert presentations** on some of these topics, **workshops** on specific topics, and **paper presentations** by the university community. This year we will have again a **fair space**, where we will share tools and novelties about free culture and software; and a poster exhibition space.

**Location**: Salón de Actos and main hall, Edificio de Gestión, Campus de Fuenlabrada

[**Access to streaming**](https://tv.urjc.es/live/event/67c850009978f33b527be5b5)

[**Registration**](https://eventos.urjc.es/126904/tickets/iv-jornadas-de-cultura-libre-de-la-urjc.html)

RAC credits will be awarded based on the number of hours of attendance, and students can attend the entire event or specific blocks. To do so, they must mark their attendance through the methods proposed during the Days. **If you participate in all the days and workshops, you can accumulate RAC credits**.

Remember that to attend the Days, you must register at [**this link**](https://eventos.urjc.es/126904/tickets/iv-jornadas-de-cultura-libre-de-la-urjc.html). Additionally, if you want to participate in a specific workshop, you must register at the corresponding link that appears in the program.
