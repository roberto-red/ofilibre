We're back with new open subjects! Until last January, we were receiving requests for second-semester subjects. And we're thrilled to have received so many and such good ones! From OfiLibre, we're very grateful, as we now have 40 new open-access subjects! The scoring is more or less the same as before, but the extension and top-scoring subjects have increased.

**The call in numbers:**

* 40 subjects
* 65 authors
* 16 note collections
* 32 presentations or slides
* 31 exercise, problem, etc. collections
* 22 evaluation test collections
* 18 short video, podcast, etc. collections
* 6 other material collections

**The call in bars:**

![](/images/2-asignaturas.png)

According to the Material Evaluation Rubric, each category could receive between 0 and 14 points. In this graph, each bar shows a category of materials (notes, slides, exercises, etc.). Within each bar, the colors indicate the amount of materials according to the score received.

Congratulations to the participating faculty! :)
