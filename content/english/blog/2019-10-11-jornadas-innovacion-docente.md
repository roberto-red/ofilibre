The VI Online Teaching Innovation Days at URJC are a space for analysis, reflection, and exchange on teaching practices and didactic methodologies applied to education supported by digital systems, services, and tools.

This year's theme will be dedicated to Teaching Platforms in Higher Education, with the goal of offering an overview of their application in the academic university setting, analyzing the impact of their implementation, evolution, and forms of use on the daily processes of teaching and learning.

On November 25, the
[Open Publishing Workshop](https://eventos.urjc.es/_files/_event/_39635/_editorFiles/file/fichas/taller_Publicacion-abierta.pdf)
will take place (from 15:30 to 19:30), aiming to provide a practical introduction to open publishing.
Although a large part of the content will focus on publishing research results, the publication of teaching materials will also be addressed.

On November 28, the expert panel "Knowledge and Free Culture for Teaching Innovation" is scheduled, where we will discuss open teaching and publishing with professors who already publish their materials openly.

Stay tuned to our blog if you want to stay informed about these days! We count on you!
