The VI URJC Online Teaching Innovation Days are a space for analysis, reflection, and exchange on teaching practices and didactic methodologies applied to education supported by digital systems, services, and tools.

This year's theme will be dedicated to Teaching Platforms in Higher Education, with the aim of providing an overview of their application in the university academic sphere, seeking to analyze the impact that their implementation, evolution, and forms of use have had on the day-to-day teaching and learning processes.

On November 25, the [Workshop on open publishing](https://eventos.urjc.es/_files/_event/_39635/_editorFiles/file/fichas/taller_Publicacion-abierta.pdf) (from 15:30 to 19:30) will take place, with which we aim to provide an introduction to open publishing, from the most practical point of view possible. Although much of the content will focus on the publication of research results, the publication of teaching materials will also be addressed.

On November 28, the expert panel "Open Knowledge and Culture for Teaching Innovation" is scheduled, in which we will debate teaching and open publishing with professors who already publish their materials openly.

Stay tuned to our blog if you want to keep informed about these days! We count on you!
