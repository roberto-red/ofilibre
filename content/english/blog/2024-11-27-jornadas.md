Free culture returns with force to the Universidad Rey Juan Carlos! In 2025, we celebrate the **IV Culture Libre Days**, an event that puts **Open Science** at the center and its impact on the generation and dissemination of knowledge.

**SAVE THE DATE:** **Wednesday, March 26 and Thursday, March 27, 2025**, at the Fuenlabrada campus, we await you to share ideas, learn, and debate about how openness and collaboration are transforming science and society.

### What will you find at these days?

During these days, the OfiLibre team and those participating in these days will guide you through an interactive and inspiring experience with a varied program that includes practical workshops, lectures, and dynamic talks. Attendees will have the opportunity to learn about open science tools, open publishing, open data management, and many other surprises.

It will also be a great opportunity to connect with other people interested in libre culture and enjoy networking moments during breaks and the fair. **Don't miss it!** 😉

We're waiting for you! 😉

  
### Pre-days: Week of Libre Knowledge and Open Science

As a prelude to the **IV Culture Libre Days**, a series of activities will take place at various Universidad Rey Juan Carlos campuses, designed to bring open science and libre culture closer, seeking to generate dynamic learning, reflection, and participation spaces.

### Week of Open Knowledge: Open Science and Libre Culture in Fuenlabrada

From **March 17 to 27, 2025**, the Fuenlabrada campus will be the scene of **interactive activities** focused on open science and libre culture. This space aims to promote interest in open access to knowledge and the relevance of sharing scientific information freely.

On **Monday, March 24, 2025**, at the Alcorcón campus, there will be a special activity dedicated to **health sciences**. The next day, **Tuesday, March 25, 2025**, will be the turn of the Vicálvaro campus, dedicated to **law and economic sciences**.

We will soon share more details about these activities. But we can tell you that with these pre-days, we aim to generate a significant impact on each campus, integrating libre culture principles into academic and professional practices. Don't miss this opportunity to be part of the change towards more libre and accessible knowledge!
