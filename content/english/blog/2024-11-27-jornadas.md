Free culture is returning in full force to Rey Juan Carlos University! In 2025 we are celebrating the **IV Free Culture Days**, an event that puts **Open Science** and its impact on the generation and dissemination of knowledge at the center.

**SAVE THE DATE:** **Wednesday 26 and Thursday 27 March 2025**, at the Fuenlabrada Campus—we look forward to sharing ideas, learning, and debating how openness and collaboration are transforming science and society.

### What will you find at these days?

During these days, the OfiLibre team and those taking part in the event will guide you through an interactive and inspiring experience with a varied program that includes hands-on workshops, talks, and dynamic presentations. Attendees will have the chance to learn about open-science tools, open publishing, open-data management, and many other surprises.

It will also be a great opportunity to connect with others interested in free culture and enjoy networking moments during breaks and the fair. **Don’t miss it!**

See you there! 😉

### Pre-Days: Open Knowledge and Open Science Week

As a prelude to the **IV Free Culture Days**, a series of activities will be held on various campuses of Rey Juan Carlos University, designed to bring open science and free culture closer together, seeking to create dynamic spaces for learning, reflection, and participation.

### Open Knowledge Week: Open Science and Free Culture in Fuenlabrada

From **17 to 27 March 2025**, the **Fuenlabrada** campus will host **interactive activities** focused on open science and free culture. The main goal of this space is to promote interest in open access to knowledge and the importance of sharing scientific information freely.

On **Monday 24 March 2025**, the **Alcorcón** campus will be the meeting point for a special activity dedicated to the **health sciences**. The next day, **Tuesday 25 March 2025**, it will be the turn of the **Vicálvaro** campus, dedicated to the **legal and economic sciences**.

We will share more details about these activities soon. What we can already tell you is that with these pre-days we aim to create a significant impact on each campus, integrating the principles of free culture into academic and professional practice. Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of the shift toward freer and more accessible knowledge!
