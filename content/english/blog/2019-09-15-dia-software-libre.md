Free Software Day is an annual international celebration of Free Software. This day is a public educational effort aimed at increasing awareness of Free Software and its advantages, and promoting its use. The Open Knowledge and Culture Office wants to celebrate this day with the entire university community; to that end, we are organizing a series of talks on Free Software that will take place on the Móstoles Campus on September 23 from 13:00 to 15:00:

* "Free FPGAs: sharing hardware", Juan González Gómez
* "OpenVidu, an open-source platform for adding videoconferencing to your web and mobile application", Pablo Fuente Pérez
* "Free Software in robotics: ROS and URJC-JdeRobot", José María Cañas Plaza
* "Enriching the open-source tool ecosystem for web testing development with Selenium", Boni García Gutiérrez
* "Damegender", David Arroyo Menéndez
* "Linux Labs at Rey Juan Carlos University: a use case", Antonio Gutiérrez Mayoral
* "The NetGUI/Netkit network emulator for active learning in Telecommunications Engineering subjects", José Centeno González
