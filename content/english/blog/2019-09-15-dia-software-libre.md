The Free Software Day is an annual international celebration of Free Software. This day is a public educational effort aimed at increasing knowledge of Free Software and its advantages, and promoting its use. OfiLibre wants to celebrate this day with the entire university community, and to that end, we are organizing a series of talks on Free Software that will take place on the Móstoles Campus on September 23rd from 13:00 to 15:00:

* "Free FPGAs: sharing hardware", Juan González Gómez
* "OpenVidu, an open source platform to add videoconferencing to your web and mobile application", Pablo Fuente Pérez
* "Free software in robotics: ROS and URJC-JdeRobot", José María Cañas Plaza
* "Enriching the ecosystem of open source tools for web development testing with Selenium", Boni García Gutiérrez
* "Damegender", David Arroyo Menéndez
* "Linux laboratories at the Rey Juan Carlos University: a use case", Antonio Gutiérrez Mayoral
* "The NetGUI/Netkit network emulator for active learning in Telecommunications Engineering subjects", José Centeno González

**[More information and registration](https://eventos.urjc.es/39175/detail/dia-del-software-libre.html)**
