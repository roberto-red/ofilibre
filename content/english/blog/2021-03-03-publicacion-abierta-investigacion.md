Open publication is an increasingly interesting option for publishing research results and other related materials. In this seminar, we will review the fundamental aspects of open publication, the options available to anyone dedicated to research, and the reasons why they may be interested in publishing openly. We will also present the URJC's position on open publication and the facilities it provides to researchers in this field.
