title: What is Free Culture?
---

It is a movement that promotes freedom to distribute and modify creative, scientific, and technical works. Its principles are:

*    Open Access: Facilitates access to information and knowledge.
*    Collaboration: Fosters cooperation between individuals and communities.
*    Sharing knowledge: Promotes the dissemination and exchange of ideas.

Examples:

*   Wikipedia: Free and collaborative encyclopedia.
*   OpenStreetMap: Worldwide editable map by anyone.
*   Free Software Projects: Linux, Apache.
