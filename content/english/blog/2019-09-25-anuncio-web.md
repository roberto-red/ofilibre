OfiLibre has been up and running for eight months, and we are finally announcing our website. On this website, you will be able to find free resources of all kinds (guides to free software, images, sounds...). Additionally, we will publish news about the world of software, licenses, publications... So that you have the most information possible when publishing or choosing media.

We will also use this medium for you to make proposals for events related to OfiLibre, or requests for guides or instructions for any free license program. Currently, we have a blog where you can read news, find out about upcoming events and much more, and you also have access to a series of files and guides to free software so that you can learn to use them and know the different alternatives that exist.

We are inaugurating, thus, our website.

**We look forward to your proposals and visits!**
