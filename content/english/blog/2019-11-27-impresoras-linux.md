Recently, new printers have been installed for use by University staff.
OfiLibre has coordinated the preparation of a [guide on how to print on them from Linux-based systems](/en/guias/impresoras).
We hope this guide will help all of us who have a Linux-based system
and want to print on this new printing service.
If you have any comments, ideas on how to improve it, or simply
want to tell us what has worked and what hasn't, [send us a message](/contacto/).

This is the first guide created with the help of the URJC community.
We hope there will be many more in the future. Will you dare to propose one?
