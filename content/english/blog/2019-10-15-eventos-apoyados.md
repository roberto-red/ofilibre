From OfiLibre we want to promote knowledge about free culture,
open publishing, free software and open data.
Therefore, since we began working, we have organized a series of informative
sessions on these topics.
But we also want to reach out to those members of our university community
who are already working on these topics and sharing your knowledge about them.
If you are interested in organizing an activity related to our areas of action
(a presentation, a workshop, a debate session, a round table, a training session, etc.)
we will try to help you.

Our support can take different forms (which will have to be considered case by case):

* We will collaborate, if that is helpful, in booking spaces on campus where the activity will take place.
* If you want your event to be recorded, we will assist in the corresponding process,
when this is possible (depending on room availability and budget).
* We will create and distribute informational posters, and carry out social media promotion,
based on the information you can give us about your activity
(what it consists of, duration, speakers...).
* We also take care of publishing the event on [Events URJC](https://eventos.urjc.es),
which allows managing registrations and other useful services to support the activity.
* We will publish the free documents used in the event on our blog,
or link to them if you prefer to publish them elsewhere.
* If you wish, we can also collaborate in the process to request
that your activity count for Academic Credit Recognition.

So if you have an idea in mind and want to carry it out,
do not hesitate to contact us: ofilibre@urjc.es.

We look forward to your ideas!!
