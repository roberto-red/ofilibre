[The Council of the Free](https://twitter.com/hashtag/ElConcilioDeLoLibre?src=hashtag_click) is a collaboration experiment with the free-software offices of the [University of Granada](https://osl.ugr.es/) and [Complutense University of Madrid](https://www.ucm.es/oficina-de-software-libre/).

The goal of this initiative is to export to other environments those activities on free knowledge that have been successful at their universities of origin.

All activities are completely free of charge and anyone can take part.
