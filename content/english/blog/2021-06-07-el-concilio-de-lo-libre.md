[The Council of Free](https://twitter.com/hashtag/ElConcilioDeLoLibre?src=hashtag_click) is a collaboration experiment with the free software offices of the [University of Granada](https://osl.ugr.es/) and the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://www.ucm.es/oficina-de-software-libre/).

The goal of this initiative is to export successful free knowledge activities from their universities of origin to other environments.

All activities are completely free and anyone can participate.

![Logos Free Software Offices](/images/blog/el-concilio-de-lo-libre/logos-3-oficinas.png)

## Calendar of Activities

#### Talk: Videoconferencing with Free Software

- Date: June 8, 2021 at 11:00h. 
- [Link to the talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YPVJpfTvvtY) (Will be available on YouTube after the event.)

<iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/YPVJpfTvvtY" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

In this talk, we will explain how to hold a professional and simple videoconference using free software. No registration is required to view it.

Talk given by [David Pacios Izquierdo](https://twitter.com/Semi_ingenieros).


#### Talk: Free Works, Use and Enjoyment
- Date: June 10, 2021 at 11:00h. 
- [Link to the videoconference](https://bbb.educar.encanarias.info/b/jes-i3l-d6m-2rr). Accessible with a web browser (Firefox, Chrome…).
- [More information.](/en/blog/trabajos-obras-libres-2/)

In this mini-workshop, we will explain what free works are and how we can use them in our work, where to find free images, audio and video, and the advantages of making our works free.

Activity led by Jesús González Barahona, from [OfiLibre](https://ofilibre.urjc.es/) of the [Rey Juan Carlos University](https://urjc.es/).

#### Talk: LaTeX with Overleaf
- Date: June 11, 2021 at 11:00h. 
- [Link to the talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GySOlqkKtDM). (Will be available on YouTube after the event.)

#### Talk: Posters with LibreOffice
- Date: June 18, 2021 at 12:00h. 
- [Link to the talk](https://jitsi.ugr.es/charla-carteles-con-libreoffice-2021-06-21).
