The Governing Council of Rey Juan Carlos University approved, at its meeting on December 18, the new Open Access and Culture Regulation. This regulation establishes the operational basis for open publishing platforms at the University, and sets up an Open Publishing Council to promote and organize their use.

The University has long had BURJC Digital, the open publishing archive managed by the Library, URJC TV, where free videos can be deposited, the virtual classroom, where subjects can be published openly, URJCx, which offers open courses, and other platforms. This regulation establishes the principles of open publishing across all these platforms, following the principles of the institutional declaration a few months ago, and the University's long track record in this area.
