OfiLibre has created a mailing list to receive all the news, updates, and events we publish on our social media immediately.

The list will be low-traffic, and we will only send useful information for those who want to receive content focused on open publication and culture.

If you have suggestions about the information you would like to see through the mailing list, you can send them to **ofilibre@urjc.es**.

**How to subscribe to the list?**

1. Send an **empty** email to **sympa@urjc.es** with the subject **"SUB lofilibre.info"**
2. Within a few seconds, you should receive an email containing the instructions you need to follow. Here, the process will be complete, and you will be subscribed.

**You don't** need to be part of the Rey Juan Carlos University to subscribe to the list.

We're waiting for you!
