OfiLibre has created a mailing list so you can receive all the news, updates, and events we publish on our social media immediately.

The list will be low-traffic; we will limit ourselves to sending useful information for anyone who wants to receive content focused on open publishing and culture.

If you have suggestions about the information you would like to see on the mailing list, you can send them to **ofilibre@urjc.es**.

**How to subscribe to the list?**

1. Send an **empty** email to **sympa@urjc.es** with the subject **"SUB lofilibre.info"**
2. A few seconds later you should receive an email containing the instructions you must follow. This will complete the process and you will be subscribed.

You **do not** need to belong to Rey Juan Carlos University to subscribe to the list.

We look forward to seeing you!
