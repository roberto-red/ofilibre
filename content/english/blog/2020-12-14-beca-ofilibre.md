At the beginning of December, the ordinary call for 2021 for 74 Collaboration Grants co-financed by Banco Santander was published on the university's electronic noticeboard, aimed at training in different services of Rey Juan Carlos University, for students enrolled at URJC.

Among these 74 Collaboration Grants, one is linked to OfiLibre, specifically to the coordination of Free Software. At OfiLibre we are looking for students from computer science and telecommunications degrees who have knowledge of free software and are eager to "tinker" and collaborate with the office in spreading free software throughout the university community.

The tasks of this grant include the following (which can be found in the call):

* Collaboration in maintaining the web infrastructure and on social media
* Collaboration in creating documents for the dissemination of knowledge, culture and free software at the university
* Collaboration in organizing events promoted by the Office
* Collaboration in attending to people interested in the Office's activities
* Collaboration in preparing documentation on the use of free software tools in different areas.

If you feel like it, go ahead and submit your application!
