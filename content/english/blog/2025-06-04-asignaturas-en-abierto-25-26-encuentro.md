The Call for Recognition of Open Access Publication of Teaching Materials for the 2025-2026 academic year has been published. This fourth call is being held at the Rey Juan Carlos University to promote the creation of high-quality open educational resources among teachers and encourage their use in the subjects they teach. The call is an initiative of the Vice-Rectorates of Digital Transformation and Teaching Innovation, Community, Campus, Culture and Sports, and Academic Planning and Teacher Training.

The details of the call can be consulted in the attached document "Call for Recognition of Open Access Publication of Teaching Materials 2025-2026". In [**this meeting**](https://tv.urjc.es/video/684038a5b0e0d0c7eb0ec512), held on Tuesday, June 3, some of its most important aspects are summarized and explained. In case of discrepancy between this summary and the call, the call will prevail, as it is the officially published document by the University.
