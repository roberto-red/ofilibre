The Call for the recognition of open-access publication of teaching materials for the 2025-2026 academic year has been published. This fourth call is held at Rey Juan Carlos University to encourage faculty to create high-quality open educational resources and promote their use in the subjects they teach. The call is an initiative of the Vice-Rectorates for Digital Transformation and Teaching Innovation, Community, Campus, Culture and Sport, and Academic Planning and Faculty Training.

The details of the call can be found in the attached document Call for the recognition of open-access publication of subjects 2025-2026. In [**this meeting**](https://tv.urjc.es/video/684038a5b0e0d0c7eb0ec512), held on Tuesday, 3 June, some of its most important aspects are summarized and explained. In the event of any discrepancy between this summary and the call, the call—officially published by the University—shall prevail.
