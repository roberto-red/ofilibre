## How to Create and Follow a Ticket in GitLab

**Step 1.** Enter http://gitlab.etsit.urjc.es and log in with your URJC account.

![step 1](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/2.png)

**Step 2.** Enter OfiLibre Plan and click on Issues.

![step 2](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/3.png)
![step 2](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/4.png)

**Step 3.** Click on New Issue.

![step 3](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/5.png)

**Step 4.** Give it a title and a description that includes the task to be performed and the conditions to close the ticket.

![step 4](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/6.png)
![step 4](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/7.png)

**Step 5 (Optional).** You can assign the task to a specific person and set a level; in OfiLibre, the different levels are: working, to start, validate, meeting, and presentation.

![step 5](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/8.png)
![step 5](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/9.png)

**Step 6.** After detailing the task, click on Submit Issue to publish it.

![step 6](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/10.png)

**Step 7.** Once the issue is published, you can track its progress. In the comments section, the latest actions performed on the task are displayed. To close the issue, click on “Close Issue”.

![step 7](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/11.png)
![step 7](/images/guias/tickets-gitlab/12.png)
