### Purpose

This call aims to promote the work of URJC teaching staff to publish their materials in open-access subjects, evaluating such work and, where appropriate, awarding an economic incentive and other benefits and effects in recognition of the effort made in preparing materials published during the 2023-24 academic year.

### Participation

Participation in this call is done by proposing subjects from the [URJC virtual classroom](https://aulavirtual.urjc.es), which must be officially taught in a URJC undergraduate or master’s degree program during the 2023-2024 academic year. The teaching materials for these subjects must have been previously published openly.

### Open-access publication of materials

Materials submitted for evaluation must [clearly display the license under which they are published](/blog/publicar-abierto/), which must be one of the open-access publication licenses approved by the URJC Open Publication Council, with the consent of all its authors ([Attribution](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.es) or [Attribution-ShareAlike](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.es) Creative Commons licenses).

Bibliographic-format materials (guides, notes, problem and exercise collections, exam collections, presentations, etc.) must be published in the institutional open repository, [BURJC Digital](https://burjcdigital.urjc.es). Videos and audios must be uploaded to [TV URJC](https://tv.urjc.es/).

Consult the document [How to publish teaching materials openly](/guias/materiales-docentes-abierto/) to understand the complete open-access publication process for teaching materials. For podcasts and videos specifically, also consult the [Procedure for publishing open-access materials on TV URJC](https://infotic.urjc.es/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=154370093).

### Submission of applications

To propose a subject, once its materials have been openly published, the person responsible for the grade book group (as listed in the Teaching Organization Plan) will fill out the [call form (first semester)](https://forms.office.com/e/gPrvVmFYah).

Subjects taught during the first semester may be submitted until September 10, 2023, and those taught during the second semester until December 30, 2023.

#### Is there a specific format for documents (presentations, text documents…)?

Although not mandatory, OfiLibre has prepared [templates that can be used for open-access published materials](/guias/plantillas-asignaturas-abierto/).

#### How do I specify the license in my documents?

Depending on the document type, the license is usually included either at the beginning or at the end. In text documents, it typically appears at the beginning where the copyright notice would normally go. In videos, it can appear at the beginning where the title appears, or at the end where the credits would appear. In audio recordings, likewise at the beginning or end. The important thing is that it appears within the material so that the license can be recognized. You can find more information in [this guide to open-access teaching material publication](/guias/materiales-docentes-abierto/#marcado-con-la-licencia-elegida) where it is explained in more detail how to include the license in the materials.

#### Who can upload a subject’s materials to the open archive?

Any of the authors can upload the materials, after signing and sending an open-access publication authorization document signed by the author uploading the materials, which serves as a declaration of consent.

#### Once the materials have the license, how are they uploaded to the open archive?

Instructions for uploading materials to the institutional open archive can be found on [this BURJC page](https://burjcdigital.urjc.es/page/howtopublish).

#### Who can submit the application for my subject in the call?

Only the person responsible for the subject’s grade book group.

#### What happens if my materials do not fit into any of the categories contemplated in the call?

In the case of materials that do not fit into any category, include them in “others” and justify appropriately.

#### Where do I put my open resources in my virtual classroom subject?

You do not need to put them anywhere in particular; it is only necessary to include the links in the participation form.

#### How do I upload my videos?

Videos and podcasts must first be uploaded to the [TV URJC](https://tv.urjc.es) platform. It is advisable to consult the [Procedure for publishing open-access materials on TV URJC](https://infotic.urjc.es/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=154370093). This procedure indicates how videos must be published on TV URJC in a single collection, which will later be indicated in the application form for this call. The procedure also shows how to embed videos uploaded to TV URJC into the Virtual Classroom.

Videos uploaded from TV URJC to the Virtual Classroom following this procedure are visible to the students enrolled in the subject. If the subject is approved to be made openly available, that collection will be opened to the general public, so anyone will be able to view it.

#### I have already submitted the form, but I want to make changes. How do I do it?

Once the form has been submitted, it cannot be modified. You can fill out the form again, and the evaluation committee will always consider the latest version submitted for each subject.

#### Can I contribute software (computer programs) that I use in my classes in any category?

Free software used in teaching can be contributed in the “Other materials” category of the call, provided it meets certain requirements. To explain what these requirements are, and in general for consultation by faculty who are authors of free software used in their classes, we have prepared specific recommendations: [Open Subjects Call: software for teaching](/guias/convocatoria-asignaturas-abierto-software).

#### How would my subject look in open access? Can I see an example?

To see a subject in open access, you can go to the Virtual Classroom and, before logging in, [several are available](https://www.aulavirtual.urjc.es/moodle/theme/urjc/code/courses.php)

#### Do I have to change the way I present my materials to participate?

Not necessarily. Although to upload them to the URJC Institutional Open Archive (BURJC Digital) they must be grouped. The idea is to keep the way we teach and make a compilation for deposit.

#### Can only CCBY and CCBYSA licenses be used? Why not CCBYNC?

The accepted licenses are CCBY and CCBYSA. CCBYNC is not accepted because it is not part of the licenses agreed upon by the Open Publication Council.

#### When is the last day to participate in the Call?

09/10/2023 is the last day to complete the call form with the required information, but to do so they must have been previously uploaded to the corresponding repositories. It is important to note that the URJC Institutional Open Archive (BURJC Digital) must be able to process the material, accept it, and assign it a handle (URL identifier). Therefore, the deadline for uploading material to BURJC Digital is September 6. If the material is rejected by the library, the handle will not be obtained and therefore participation will not be possible at that point.

In the call, point 2 states:

 “The following data must be clearly included in the header, first page or equivalent: Authors, title, and date. It must also include a reference to the URJC, to the subject or subjects in which the material is used and to the place of deposit (TV URJC or BURJC digital, including its link) and clearly the open-access distribution license”. 

#### So, when it says “including its link,” what does it mean?

The digital archive itself will handle this process. When the material is included in the archive and the link (handle) is generated, the archive itself will include this information in the deposited material.

#### I want to publish a set of exercises, but they are often inherited from years and years… can I use them and upload them to the repository?

The call is designed to release and publish self-created materials, or co-authored with authorization from the other authors. Publishing something as one’s own without being sure whether we are the authors or not is not advisable.

#### Until last year, two of us were teaching the subject. This year, only the other professor will teach it. Can I participate in the call?

No. The call is for faculty teaching it this year. On the other hand, if the materials (which may be co-authored with other professors) finally receive economic recognition, it will be for the professors teaching the subject in the current academic year.

#### If I have my videos on YouTube, does it count for the call?

No. They must be in the URJC-TV repository with one of the proposed licenses.

#### I have a computer program that I would like to participate in the call. How do I include it?

Computer programs can indeed participate in the call. There is a procedure for their storage, and in the participation form, they must be included in the “Computer programs” category.

#### If I teach classes in the second semester… can I participate now?

No, you must wait for the second-semester call.

#### I have uploaded videos to TV URJC and I don’t see them available

This is normal, because the videos are uploaded but not yet public. They will become public if your subject is finally made open. Until then, only your subject’s students will be able to see them, accessing them via a link from the virtual classroom.

#### I have Notes and Slides, how do I deposit them?

As they are two PDF documents (each corresponding to a category), they would have to be two entries in BURJC Digital, one for each document.

#### When uploading my notes to BURJC… do I upload them by topic or by type of material?

In this call, documents must be uploaded as a single PDF per category. This does not prevent you from using them topic by topic in your classes, and having one PDF per topic (or per class session, or whatever works best for you). But when uploading to BURJC Digital, it must be a single document. Later, somewhere visible in the subject, you will link to that document (for example, “complete collection of notes”), so that when the subject is made open, it is clear where the complete document is.

The complete document can simply be a concatenation of the PDFs (if you need help composing that concatenation, let us know), if possible with a cover page and an index. Or, depending on the tool you use to write them, all the notes in a single joint document, from which you will produce a single PDF. But then, if you prefer to upload the materials topic by topic for your students, because that’s how you manage them better (or because you make small changes to fix errors you notice when preparing each week’s sessions), there is no problem with that. Just advise your students that it is better to use the topic-by-topic versions.

#### Is the maximum personal economic incentive for the entire academic year?

The maximum of 1,500 per person is for the entire academic year. This means, for example, that if the personal maximum has already been reached with first-semester subjects, no economic incentive will be received if participating in subjects submitted in the second semester. In any case, if this maximum is reached, the other benefits of the call will continue to apply, so you can participate in the call with second-semester subjects even if the personal maximum has already been reached during the first semester.

#### For an annual subject, in which semester can it be submitted?

You can submit the subject in the call for either semester, but only in one of them. In any case, the entire subject will be submitted.
