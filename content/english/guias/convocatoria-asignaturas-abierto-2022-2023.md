### Purpose

This call aims to promote the work of URJC teaching staff to publish their materials in open-access subjects, evaluating such work and, where appropriate, assigning an economic incentive and other benefits and effects, recognizing the effort made in developing materials published during the 2022-23 academic year.

### Participation

Participation in this call is done by proposing subjects from the [URJC virtual classroom](https://aulavirtual.urjc.es), which must be official teaching in a URJC undergraduate or master's degree during the 2022-2023 academic year. The teaching materials for these subjects must have been previously published openly.

### Publication of materials in open access

Materials submitted for evaluation must clearly display the license under which they are published, which must be one of the open-access publication licenses approved by the URJC Open Publishing Council, with the consent of all authors ([Creative Commons Attribution](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.es) or [Attribution-ShareAlike](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.es) licenses).

Bibliographic-format materials (guides, notes, problem and exercise collections, exam collections, presentations, etc.) must be published in the institutional open repository, [BURJC Digital](https://burjcdigital.urjc.es). Videos and audios must be uploaded to [TV URJC](https://tv.urjc.es/).

Once the materials are published, they must be linked from the corresponding virtual classroom subject in a clearly visible location so that it can be easily verified that the openly published materials are referenced in the subject.

You can consult the document [How to publish teaching materials openly](/guias/materiales-docentes-abierto/) to understand the complete process of openly publishing teaching materials. For audios and videos specifically, you should also consult the [Procedure for publishing open materials on TV URJC](https://infotic.urjc.es/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=154370093).

### Application submission

To propose a subject, once its materials have been published openly, the grade-book group responsible (as listed in the Teaching Organization Plan) will complete the call form (first semester) (call now closed) or the [call form (second semester)](https://forms.office.com/e/v623XaKDSk).

Subjects taught during the first semester can be submitted until September 30, 2022, and those taught during the second semester until January 20, 2023.

### Resolving doubts

To resolve doubts related to this call, you can consult the frequently asked questions, with their answers, later in this document. If they cannot be resolved this way, they can be raised by email to ofilibre@urjc.es, where they will be addressed as soon as possible.

### Frequently asked questions, with their answers {#faq}

To facilitate consultation, any doubts that are resolved will be included in this section.


#### Is there a specific format for documents (presentations, text documents...)?

Although not mandatory, [OfiLibre](https://ofilibre.urjc.es) has prepared [templates that can be used for openly published materials](/guias/plantillas-asignaturas-abierto/).


#### Which licenses are valid?

There are two licenses approved by the URJC Open Publishing Council as valid for open materials:

* [Creative Commons BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.es)

* [Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.es).

Both licenses are explained in more detail in the [Guide on publishing teaching materials in open access](/guias/materiales-docentes-abierto/).


#### How do I specify the license in my documents?

Depending on the type of document, the license is usually included either at the beginning or at the end. In text documents, it usually appears at the beginning where the copyright notice would normally go. In videos, it can appear at the beginning, where the title appears, or at the end where the credits would appear. In audio recordings, likewise at the beginning or end. The important thing is that it appears within the material so that the license can be recognized. You can find more information in [this guide to publishing open teaching materials]({{< ref "/guias/materiales-docentes-abierto#marcado-con-la-licencia-elegida" >}}) where it is explained in more detail how to include the license in the materials.


#### Who can upload a subject's materials to the open archive?

The materials can be uploaded by any of the authors, after signing and sending an open publication authorization document signed by the author uploading the materials, which serves as a declaration of consent.
        

#### Once the materials have the license, how are they uploaded to the open archive?

Instructions for uploading materials to the institutional open archive can be found [on this BURJC page](https://burjcdigital.urjc.es/page/howtopublish).

  
#### Who can submit the application for my subject in the call?

The person responsible for the subject in the 2022-2023 academic year must submit the application.


#### What happens if my materials do not fit into any of the categories contemplated in the call?

For materials that do not fit into any category, include them in others and justify it appropriately.


#### Where do I put my open resources in my virtual classroom subject?

For the call, place the links to the open resources in an easily accessible location, such as at the top of the content page, regardless of whether they are later linked from other parts of the subject. This will facilitate access to them during the application evaluation process. Keep in mind that evaluators must easily find the links to these materials.

#### How do I upload my videos and link them from Virtual Classroom?

Videos and podcasts must first be uploaded to the [TV URJC](https://tv.urjc.es) platform and then linked so they are visible in the Virtual Classroom subject. It is advisable to consult the [Procedure for publishing open materials on TV URJC](https://infotic.urjc.es/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=154370093). This procedure indicates how videos must be published on TV URJC in a single collection, which will later be indicated in the application form for this call. The procedure also shows how to insert videos uploaded to TV URJC into Virtual Classroom.

Videos uploaded from TV URJC to Virtual Classroom following this procedure are visible to the students of the subject. If the subject is approved to be made openly available, that collection will be opened to the general public, so everyone will be able to see it.


#### I have already submitted the form, but I want to make changes. How do I do it?

Once the form is submitted, it cannot be modified. You can fill out the form again, and the evaluation committee will always consider the last version submitted for each subject.

#### Can I include software (computer programs) that I use in my classes in any category?

Free software used in teaching can be included in the "Other materials" category of the call, if it meets certain requirements. To explain what these requirements are, and in general for consultation by lecturers who are authors of free software used in their classes, we have prepared specific recommendations: [Open Subjects Call: software for teaching"](/guias/convocatoria-asignaturas-abierto-software)

#### Can only CCBY and CCBYSA licenses be used? Why not CCBYNC?
R: The accepted licenses are CCBY and CCBYSA. CCBYNC is not accepted because it is not part of the licenses agreed by the Open Publishing Council.

#### When is the last day to participate in the Call?
09/30/2022 is the last day to complete the call form with the required information, but to do so they must have been previously uploaded to the corresponding repositories. It is important to take into account that the URJC Institutional Open Archive (BURJC Digital) must be able to process the material, accept it and give it a handle (url identifier). Therefore, the deadline for uploading material to BURJC Digital is September 28.
If the material is rejected by the library, the handle will not be obtained and therefore participation will not be possible at that point.

#### In the call, point 2 says the following: “They must clearly include in the header, first page or equivalent the following data: Authors, title, and date. #### I want to publish a package of exercises, but many times they are inherited from years and years… can I use them and upload them to the repository?
The call is intended to release and publish materials of one's own creation, or co-authored with authorization from the remaining authors. Publishing something as one's own, without being sure whether we are the authors or not, is not advisable.

#### Until last year we were two lecturers teaching the subject. For this course only the other lecturer will teach it. Can I participate in the call?
No. Those who teach the subject this year participate in the call. On the other hand, the materials (which can be co-authored with other lecturers) if they finally receive economic recognition, will be for the lecturers teaching the subject in the current academic year.

#### If I teach classes in the second semester... can I participate now?
No, you have to wait for the second-semester call.

#### When uploading my notes to BURJC… do I upload them by topic or by type of material?

Complete question: The call says: In the case of bibliographic materials (notes, presentations or slides, problem collections, exam collections), they must be deposited in the URJC Institutional Open Archive (BURJC Digital), as a single document per category, in PDF format and in the source format used for their creation (as an attached file to the document).
Can't I upload the notes by each topic? (That's how I have them made and uploaded to BURJC, because it seems more pedagogical that each topic has its videos, its notes, its solved exercises, etc.). Similarly, the exercises are by each topic (and even different files for each exercise).

Answer: In this call you must upload the documents
in a single PDF per category. This does not prevent you from using them topic by topic in your classes, and having a PDF per topic (or per class session, or whatever you find best). But at the time of uploading to BURJC Digital, it must be a single document. Then, in some visible part of the subject you will link to that document (it could be, for example, "complete collection of notes"), so that when the subject is made openly available it can be clearly seen where the complete document is.

The complete document can simply be a concatenation of the PDFs (if you need help composing that concatenation, let us know), if possible with a cover and index. Or, depending on the tool you use to write them, all the notes in a joint document, from which you will produce a single PDF.
But then, if you prefer to upload the materials topic by topic for your students, because you handle them better that way (or because you make small changes to fix errors you detect when preparing each week's sessions), there is no problem with that. Just advise your students that it's better to use the topic-by-topic versions.

#### Is the maximum personal economic incentive for the entire academic year?

Complete question: The call text indicates that there is a maximum personal economic incentive of 1,500 euros. Is this maximum per semester, or for the entire academic year?

Answer: The maximum of 1,500 per person is for the entire academic year. This means, for example, that if the personal maximum has already been reached with first-semester subjects, no economic incentive will be received if participating in subjects submitted in the second semester. In any case, if this maximum is reached, the other benefits of the call will continue to apply, so you can participate in the call with second-semester subjects even if the personal maximum has already been reached during the first semester.

#### For an annual subject, in which semester can it be submitted?

Complete question: My subject is not semester-based, but annual. In which semester call can I submit it?

Answer: You can submit the subject in the call for either of the two semesters, but only in one of them. In any case, the entire subject will be submitted.
