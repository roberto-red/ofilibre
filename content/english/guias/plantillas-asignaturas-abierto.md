These templates are provided in [LibreOffice](https://es.libreoffice.org/) format, an open-source office suite available for Windows, Linux, and MacOS operating systems, which uses the standard [Open Document](https://opendocumentformat.org/) format to save the documents we create with this free suite, but is also compatible and can open Microsoft Office formats (among others).

Two templates are provided in LibreOffice format:

* [Text document template](/documentos/plantillas/libreoffice-writer-plantilla-urjc-v2.ott). It has two styles: the first page includes the complete URJC logo, including the university name, and is oriented to be the title page of the document. The second and subsequent pages only have the logo, and are oriented to be the content of the document.
* [Presentation template](/documentos/plantillas/libreoffice-impress-plantilla-urjc-v2.otp). Based on the official templates for presentations from the university, but adapted to LibreOffice format, with institutional colors. This template has two styles for the document title (red and black) and different styles for the content depending on the text and/or image layout needed.

To use the templates, simply open them with LibreOffice, and when saving, select "Save As..." > "ODF Presentation (odp)" for presentations, or "Save As..." > "ODF Text Document (odt)" for text documents, as indicated in the image.
