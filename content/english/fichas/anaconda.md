Anaconda is a free and open-source distribution of the Python and R languages, used in data science and machine learning. This includes processing large volumes of information, predictive analysis, and scientific computing. It is aimed at simplifying the deployment and management of software packages.
The different package versions are managed by the conda package management system, which makes it quite easy to install, run, and update data science and machine learning software such as Scikit-team, TensorFlow, and SciPy.
