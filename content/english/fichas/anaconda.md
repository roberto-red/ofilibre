Anaconda is a free and open distribution of the Python and R languages, used in data science and machine learning. This includes processing large volumes of information, predictive analysis, and scientific computing. It is designed to simplify the deployment and management of software packages.
The different versions of the packages are managed through the conda package management system, which makes it quite simple to install, run, and update data science and machine learning software such as Scikit-team, TensorFlow, and SciPy.
