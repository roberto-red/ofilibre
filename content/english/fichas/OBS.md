[Open Broadcaster Software (OBS)](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Broadcaster_Software) is a free and open-source application for video recording and live streaming over the internet.

It allows the capture and mixing of video and audio sources in real time, scene composition, transitions between scenes, encoding, recording, and retransmission.

It supports the RTMP protocol to enable data transmission, with presets for different streaming platforms such as YouTube, Twitch, Hitbox, or DailyMotion. It features simplified panels to configure the interface for each type of recording, and extensive filter support for use in video editing and production.
