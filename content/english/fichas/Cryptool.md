CrypTool is a free software that illustrates cryptographic concepts. It is the most widely used electronic learning program in the world in the field of cryptography. There are over 60 algorithms implemented extensively, efficiently, and with a large number of algorithms and tools for analysis. The graphical interface and abundant online information allow the user, even an Internet novice, to delve into the mysteries of cryptography. Classic mechanisms, as well as asymmetric RSA cryptography, elliptic curve-based cryptography, digital signatures, or the Diffie-Hellman key exchange protocol, are explained using animations. It also contains a tutorial on prime numbers and elementary number theory.
