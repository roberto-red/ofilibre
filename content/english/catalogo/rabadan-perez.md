## Process

* Material selection: The material design is based on the contents collected from the teaching guides of the subjects and usually references the books of the recently deceased Professor don Javier Martín-Pliego, who gave his express consent to create these videos. Other CC-licensed material, such as Wikipedia content, has also been used. All links to the used material can be found in the videos themselves.

* The audio/video design was done using Apple's proprietary Logic Pro and Final Cut X software, with an academic use license in my name.

* Process and generation of free material: The main difficulty was the process of reorganizing the contents with a coherent teaching discourse, the time required to design the slides, record the classes, and subsequent editing. This workload has allowed me to discover YouTube's comment platform as a "feedback" from users of the content to improve and adapt future versions.
