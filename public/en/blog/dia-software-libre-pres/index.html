Presentations of the Free Software Day

Free Software
  • 25 Oct, 2019

We celebrated the Day of Free Software with the University Community on September 23rd. To do so, we organized a series of talks on different aspects of free software, as we use or produce it at the URJC. For those who couldn’t attend, and for those who want to remember this day, here are some photographs of the event and the presentations (slides) used by the speakers:

“Free FPGAs: sharing hardware”, Juan González Gómez

OpenVidu, an open source platform to add video conferencing to your web and mobile application, Pablo Fuente Pérez

Free software in robotics: ROS and URJC-JdeRobot", José María Cañas Plaza

“Enriching the ecosystem of open source tools for web development testing with Selenium”, Boni García Gutiérrez

“Damegender”, David Arroyo Menéndez

“Linux laboratories at the Rey Juan Carlos University: a use case”, Antonio Gutiérrez Mayoral

“The NetGUI/Netkit network emulator for active learning in Telematic Engineering subjects”, José Centeno González

We look forward to seeing you at our next activities!
