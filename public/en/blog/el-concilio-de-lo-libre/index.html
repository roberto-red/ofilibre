The Council of the Free

University Free Culture
  • 07 Jun, 2021

“Sharing knowledge is the key to a free and just society.”

The Council of Free is a collaboration experiment with the free software offices of the University of Granada and the Complutense University of Madrid.

The goal of this initiative is to export successful free knowledge activities from their universities of origin to other environments.

All activities are completely free and anyone can participate.

Calendar of Activities

Talk: Videoconferencing with Free Software

  • Date: June 8, 2021 at 11:00h.
  • Link to the talk (Will be available on YouTube after the event.)

In this talk, we will explain how to hold a professional and simple videoconference using free software. No registration is required to view it.

Talk given by David Pacios Izquierdo.

Talk: Free Works, Use and Enjoyment

  • Date: June 10, 2021 at 11:00h.
  • Link to the videoconference. Accessible with a web browser (Firefox, Chrome…).
  • More information.

In this mini-workshop, we will explain what free works are and how we can use them in our work, where to find free images, audio and video, and the advantages of making our works free.

Activity led by Jesús González Barahona, from OfiLibre of the Rey Juan Carlos University.

Talk: LaTeX with Overleaf

  • Date: June 11, 2021 at 11:00h.
  • Link to the talk. (Will be available on YouTube after the event.)

In this talk, you will learn how to optimize the writing of your teaching materials, notes, scientific articles, TFG/TFM or thesis.

Given by David Pacios Izquierdo.

Talk: Posters with LibreOffice

  • Date: June 18, 2021 at 12:00h.
  • Link to the talk.

In this talk, we will explain how to create posters in a very simple way using LibreOffice.

Given by Juan Sánchez Rodríguez.

Cover and posters by: Juan Sánchez
