OfiLibre recipes: From recording in Teams to free video

Open Data Resources
  • 11 Dec, 2020

A very common scenario in these times of distance learning is having recorded a class that took place via videoconference, and wanting to generate a free video from it, which we can upload to URJC TV or any video publishing platform (YouTube, Vimeo, PeerTube, etc.).

This video recipe focuses on this scenario. We start with a video that has been recorded using the option provided by Teams, and is available on Stream (as usually happens with our URJC configuration). Using Stream itself (to choose the part of the video that interests us), and free tools (LibreOffice, OpenShot), we will produce a video similar to this one, which allows students to better understand what they will see in it (thanks to a small header), and makes it clear that it is a free video that can be shared (thanks to a license and credits included at the end).
